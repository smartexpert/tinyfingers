TinyFingers Deluxe

A safe keyboard playground for very small humans.

Hand the keyboard over. Every key makes a colour, a shape and a friendly note. Nothing gets deleted, nothing gets sent anywhere, and your little one cannot wander off into the rest of the computer.

Before you start
Checking what this browser can lock down…
  • Play goes fullscreen and swallows keyboard shortcuts. If fullscreen is lost, an overlay appears and the next key or tap re-locks it automatically.
  • Nothing on the web can block Ctrl+Alt+Del, and on some systems Alt+F4 or the power button still work. For absolute lockdown, pair this with your operating system's kiosk or guided-access mode.
  • To get out, a grown-up types the 4-digit code shown in the corner — or presses and holds that badge for 2 seconds.

Works offline. No accounts, no uploads, no ads.
